Class Formats

What each class actually involves

Mat Pilates

45 minutes of bodyweight core, mobility and control work on a mat, with light props (bands, small balls). Best starting point for beginners.

Reformer

50 minutes on the spring-resistance reformer machine. Full-body strength work with hands-on spotting — machines are provided.

Barre Fusion

45 minutes combining Pilates principles with barre-style isometric holds, set to music. A different kind of burn, same attention to form.

FAQ

Before your first class

What should I bring to my first class?

Grip socks (required for Reformer, available for $12 at the front desk), a water bottle, and comfortable, fitted clothing. Mats are provided.

Do I need experience to book Reformer?

No — every Reformer class includes a two-minute machine orientation for new students before class begins.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel or move your booking up to 8 hours before class with no charge. Later cancellations forfeit the class credit.

Is parking available?

Yes — free lot parking directly behind the studio, plus street parking on Willow Lane.
