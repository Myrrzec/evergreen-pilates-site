Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Evergreen Pilates Studio collects through this website and how it's used.

What we collect

When you submit the contact/booking form, we collect the information you provide: name, email, phone number (optional), class interest, and any message you write. We also use Google Analytics to understand general site traffic, such as which pages are visited and how users arrive at the site.

How we use it

Form submissions are used only to respond to your booking request or question. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. Analytics data is used in aggregate to improve the website and is not linked to your personal booking information.

Third-party services

This site uses Formspree to process contact form submissions and Google Analytics for site traffic analysis. Each service processes data under its own privacy policy in addition to this one.

Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain booking records, and can be deleted on request.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your contact information at any time by emailing hello@evergreenpilates.example. Most browsers also let you block analytics cookies directly in your settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@evergreenpilates.example.
