Programs

What we train

From equipment-based Pilates to strength and cardio: pick one program or combine several.

Balance Body · Allegro

Pilates Reformer

Pilates training on the Allegro reformer (Balanced Body, USA). It builds a muscular “corset” that supports flawless posture, and develops functional strength, spinal flexibility, joint mobility, stretch range, coordination and balance.

  • Stronger back
  • Better posture and spine health
  • Spine and joints protected from strain
  • Suitable for any age
  • No injuries or negative side effects
  • Works the stabiliser muscles
  • Toned, lean body
  • Muscular endurance and coordination

Pilates Functional

A program with balls, isotonic rings and rollers. It builds a muscular corset that helps keep the natural curves of the spine.

Pilates MVE Chair

The MVE Chair trainer improves the work of the muscles that hold the spine upright while seated.

Pilates MOTR Roll

A multi-function roll with resistance cords: work in eight planes with three resistance levels. Strengthens deep muscles.

Stretch for Men

Elasticity of ligaments and large muscles, joint mobility and spinal flexibility. Hammock stretching, Pilates Reformer and the Gyrotonic® method on the Pulley Tower.

Power · Cardio · Functional

Power strengthens the large muscles and shapes the body, Cardio builds heart endurance, Functional teaches safe everyday movement.

Pilates Mat I

An entry-level course in the Pilates system without equipment: technique, breathing, neutral spine and core exercises.
